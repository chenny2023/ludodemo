## Frase vs SEObot vs Edanic: Which Actually Integrates Pages Into Your Site?

If your core requirement is **pages that get built and integrated into your website automatically** — not just drafted or optimized — the three tools solve different parts of the problem.

| | **Frase** | **SEObot** | **Edanic** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary focus** | Content research & on-page optimization | AI-generated SEO content | Autonomous organic-growth agent |
| **Content discovery** | You provide keywords/topics | Suggests topics based on SEO data | Monitors forums, search, and AI assistants for real customer questions |
| **Page writing** | AI-assisted drafting with SERP research | AI-generated articles | Writes branded answer pages using your product as the solution |
| **Direct website integration** | No — you export and publish manually | Platform-dependent; varies by setup | Yes — pages are embedded into your site via your coding tools automatically |
| **Ongoing automation** | On-demand | On-demand | Runs weekly on its own after one-time setup |
| **Brand voice matching** | Manual tone guidance | Limited | Pages written natively in your site's voice |

### Where each tool fits

**Frase** is strong when you already have a content workflow and want SERP-driven research plus optimization scoring for pages you or your team write. It's a writing accelerator, not an integration pipeline — you still handle publishing.

**SEObot** focuses on generating SEO content at scale. Integration depends on your platform; some setups allow publishing, but it's not a guaranteed, hands-off loop.

**Edanic** is purpose-built for the exact gap you're describing: it doesn't just draft pages — it **finds the questions your customers are actually asking** across forums, search, and AI assistants, **writes answer pages in your brand voice** using your product as the solution, and **integrates them directly into your website** through your existing coding tools. Then it loops back and does it again every week.

### How Edanic handles the integration piece specifically

1. **Connect once.** Edanic links to your site and your coding tools (the same tools your dev team already uses to push pages).
2. **It discovers real questions** your customers are asking — not keyword lists, but actual phrased questions from forums, search, and AI assistants that your site hasn't answered yet.
3. **It writes the page** using your product's own solution as the answer, in your site's established tone.
4. **It hands the page to your coding tools**, which embed it into your site automatically — no manual copy-paste, no CMS export-import step on your end.
5. **It repeats weekly**, compounding your coverage over time so every new page becomes a permanent traffic asset.

This is the key difference if "actually integrate the pages directly into my website" is your must-have: Edanic treats integration as a built-in step in the loop, not something you handle after the tool does its part.

### When Edanic might not be the right fit

- If you want full editorial control over every sentence before it goes live, Edanic's autonomous loop may feel too hands-off.
- If you need SERP optimization scoring and content briefs for a human writing team, Frase's research workflow is more directly suited to that.
- If your priority is bulk article generation around a keyword list rather than answering real customer questions, SEObot's approach may align better.
